Pick your path

Both paths use Package 11. The difference is where your market data comes from.

Not sure? If your account comes from a prop firm, use Rithmic. Sierra's lower Denali price needs a live, funded futures account, and prop firm evaluation accounts do not qualify.
01

Create a Sierra Chart account

  1. Open sierrachart.com/RegisterStep1.php and create an account.
  2. Write down your Sierra Chart username. You log in to the software with it. Studies you buy from other people are also linked to it.
02

Download and install

  1. On sierrachart.com, open Main » Software Download and download the installer.
  2. Run it. Install to C:\SierraChart. Do not install under your Desktop or Documents.
  3. Start Sierra Chart and log in with your Sierra Chart username and password.
  4. Update to the newest version: Help » Download Current Version. Sierra restarts when it is done.
03

Activate Service Package 11

  1. Log in to the Account Control Panel.
  2. Add funds on Make a Payment.
  3. Open Activate Services and choose Service Package 11 · Integrated with Advanced Features.

04

Connect your data feed

Before Sierra

  1. Find your Rithmic username, password and server name in your prop firm's dashboard or welcome email.
  2. Sign the Rithmic agreements once. Your firm gives you the link, or you log in to Rithmic R|Trader once. Without the agreements Sierra shows "permission denied".

In Sierra Chart

  1. Open Global Settings » Data/Trade Service Settings.
  2. In Current Selected Service, choose Rithmic Direct - DTC [Trading]. Press OK.
  3. Wait 10 seconds. Open Data/Trade Service Settings again. The Server list now fills.
  4. Choose the server your prop firm names. "Paper Trading" servers are for evaluation and sim accounts.
  5. Type the Rithmic username and password into Trading, Market Data and Historical Data. Use the same pair in all three. Type them by hand. They are case sensitive.
  6. Press OK. Then File » Connect to Data Feed.
Sierra's own docs say Rithmic's market depth has known issues in Sierra Chart. If a depth tool looks thin or frozen, check the feed first.

Sierra's full instructions: Rithmic setup.

On the Sierra Chart website

  1. Add enough funds to cover the package and the exchange fee: Make a Payment.
  2. Sign the CME agreements in the Account Control Panel: the Uniform Subscriber Agreement and the CME Non Professional Certification. Sign both in the same session. For non-professional pricing, enter your funded futures account number and clearing firm.
  3. On the Denali Data Feed Exchanges page, activate CME with Market Depth. Pick an option that says "with Market Depth". Without it you get no order book.

In Sierra Chart

  1. Open Global Settings » Data/Trade Service Settings » Common Settings. Set Allow Support for Sierra Chart Data Feeds to Yes.
  2. In Current Selected Service, choose SC Data, or your broker's trading service if you trade through Sierra.
  3. Press OK. Then File » Reconnect.
  4. Check that [M] shows after the symbol on your chart. That is Denali data.
The lower non-professional price needs a live, funded futures account that Sierra Chart connects to at least once a month. Without it you get delayed data. Prop firm evaluation accounts do not qualify.

Sierra's full instructions: Denali Exchange Data Feed.

05

Open an ES chart and check the data

  1. File » New Chartbook.
  2. File » Find Symbol. Search ES and pick the E-mini S&P 500 futures. The symbol looks different on each feed, so pick the ES entry your feed lists.
  3. Chart » Chart Settings, Symbol section: set the continuous futures contract to Volume Based Rollover, Back Adjusted. The chart then follows the most traded contract and the history stays continuous across rolls.
  4. For any tool that reads the order book: Global Settings » Symbol Settings, find ES and set Record Market Depth Data to Yes.
  5. File » Save the chartbook.
Price moving and the clock ticking on the chart means you are connected. If not, open Window » Message Log. The answer is almost always in the last few lines.
